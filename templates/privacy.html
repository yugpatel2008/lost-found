1. Introduction

Welcome to the Indus Uni. Lost & Found Portal. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide while using our platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Basic account details like name, email, and university ID.
  • Lost or found item descriptions, photos, and locations.
  • Device data such as IP address, browser type, and access times.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your lost/found items to the community.
  • To notify users about matched items.
  • To improve platform functionality and user experience.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. We may only share details with Indus Uni. authorities in case of sensitive or escalated issues.

5. Data Security

Your data is stored securely and only accessible to authorized users and administrators. We use reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access.

6. Your Choices

You can modify or delete your posts at any time. If you'd like to delete your account or have privacy concerns, please contact the support team.

7. Updates to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated occasionally. Any major changes will be communicated via the platform or email notifications.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, please reach out to our support team at support@lostfoundportal.com.
